# Contact SkyLens — Get in touch

SkyLens has no accounts or dashboards to log into — if something needs a human, this is where to find one.

## Channels

- **General & support** — Questions about a report, something that looks wrong, or how a check works. support@skyphr.com
- **Privacy** — Questions about how SkyLens handles data — see the [Privacy Policy](https://lens.skyphr.com/privacy) for the full details. privacy@skyphr.com
- **Business & partnerships** — Press, partnerships, or anything about Skyphr, the company behind SkyLens. hello@skyphr.com

SkyLens is built and supported by Skyphr, so every message above reaches the Skyphr team. For anything beyond SkyLens itself, visit https://skyphr.com
